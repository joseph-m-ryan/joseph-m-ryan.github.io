abstract: >-
  We study first-passage timing statistics for quantum trajectories conditioned on measurement
  outcomes of a harmonic oscillator subject to classical additive noise. Projective measurements and
  energy quantization produce substantial differences between the quantum and classical
  first-passage-time distributions at low thresholds, with these differences diminishing at higher
  energies. Using both density-matrix and stochastic pure-state approaches, we find consistent timing
  statistics and observe quantization effects in the phase-space distributions, revealing
  measurement-induced nonclassical features and providing a framework for studying
  quantum-to-classical transitions in monitored systems.
