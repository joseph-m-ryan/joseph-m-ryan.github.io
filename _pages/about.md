I'm a PhD candidate in physics at the [Duke Quantum Center](https://quantum.duke.edu/), where I build and run trapped-ion experiments in [Crystal Noel's group](https://noellab.pratt.duke.edu/).

I work on **quantum stochastic processes**: how noise and measurement shape the dynamics of trapped ions. My recent work includes the first experimental measurement of quantum first-passage-time distributions, the quantum–classical crossover in noisy monitored oscillators, and the heating and escape of trapped atoms driven by colored noise.

Before Duke, I earned a B.S. in Honors Physics & Mathematics from the University of Michigan, where I worked with the Aidala group on the LHCb experiment at CERN.
